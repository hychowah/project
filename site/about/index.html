    About

    Background

    I’m Eric Chow, a robotics engineer with a mechanical engineering background from HKUST and an MSc in Mechanical and Automation Engineering from CUHK. I’ve worked in consumer product development, research robotics and construction robotics.

    My work connects mechanisms, electronics, embedded firmware and robot software. At C3, I took a suspended painting robot from proof of concept through field deployment and end-effector redesign. That experience shaped how I approach system interfaces, operator workflows and troubleshooting on physical hardware.

    Career Timeline

    Technical Engineer — Mega Precision Technology (Dongguan) Limited (Aug 2026 – Present)

    I investigate how robotic technologies can be integrated into manufacturing processes. My work combines technical research, engineering concept development and prototype evaluation to assess potential applications and guide integration decisions.

    Senior Mechanical Engineer — C3 Construction Robotics Limited (Mar 2024 – Aug 2026)

    Lead engineer on a suspended painting robot for industrial tanks, through prototyping, field deployment and roller-end-effector redesign. My responsibilities covered mechanical design, electronics, robot software, system integration and operator tools, supported by software testing and field troubleshooting.

    Research Assistant — The Chinese University of Hong Kong (Dec 2022 – Mar 2024)

    Developed control software for a cable-suspended inspection platform and a cable-actuated flexible cleaning hose. The work involved hose modelling, coordinated actuation and testing on construction sites. Co-inventor of a granted US patent.

    Engineering Trainee — Continental Conair Limited (Jan 2019 – Jan 2021)

    Developed mechanical concepts and prototypes for personal-care appliances, combining ergonomic and performance considerations with plastic-part design and manufacturing constraints. Worked with other engineers to refine designs for assembly and production.

    Engineering Skills

    • Robotics software and control: I develop Python and C++ software for physical robots, coordinate subsystems through ROS2, and build Qt-based operator interfaces for telemetry and editable robot workflows.
    • Hardware integration: My work connects sensors, embedded controllers and actuators, including the interfaces, diagnostics and recovery behaviour needed to test and operate robotic systems.
    • Mechanical design and prototyping: I use SolidWorks for mechanisms and structural design, supported by structural analysis, design for manufacture and assembly, and physical prototype testing.

    My personal INO AI tutor combines a FastAPI backend, a React/TypeScript interface and an optional Discord bot. I built typed LLM tool calls, adaptive review workflows and local multilingual concept search, with one SQLite database for learning history and application state.
