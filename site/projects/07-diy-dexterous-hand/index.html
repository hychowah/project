    DIY Dexterous Hand

    Dexterous-hand design in Onshape and early 3D-printed finger prototyping, with a planned build using Unitree J288 servo motors.

    Overview

    Personal DIY project · 2026–Present

    Work in progress: mechanical design and early finger prototyping.

    I am developing a DIY dexterous hand in Onshape, with plans to use Unitree J288 servo motors for actuation.

    Design And Prototyping

    The CAD work brings the fingers, thumb and palm into a hand assembly. I have also made a 3D-printed finger prototype as part of the mechanical development.

    The next steps are to refine the full-hand design and work towards actuator integration, assembly and motion testing. I will update this page as the project develops.

    Design tool: Onshape
    Planned actuation: Unitree J288 servo motors
