    DIY Dexterous Hand

    Dexterous-hand design in Onshape and early 3D-printed finger prototyping, with a Python control bench for planned Unitree J288 actuation.

    Project focus

    Overview

    Personal DIY project · 2026–Present

    Work in progress: mechanical design, early finger prototyping and actuator-control software.

    I am developing a DIY dexterous hand in Onshape, with plans to use Unitree J288 servo motors for actuation.

    Design And Prototyping

    The CAD work brings the fingers, thumb and palm into a hand assembly. I have also made a 3D-printed finger prototype as part of the mechanical development.

    The next steps are to refine the full-hand design, integrate the actuators into the finger mechanisms, and work towards assembly and hand-level motion testing.

    Tools: Onshape, Python and Textual

    Planned actuation: Unitree J288 servo motors

    Actuator Control Software

    Alongside the mechanical work, I am developing a Python bench-control tool for the J288 servos to support actuator setup and motion checks before full-hand integration.

    • Commands and feedback: a terminal dashboard for position, speed and torque commands, gain adjustment, and live motor feedback, temperature and fault monitoring.
    • Motion checks: configurable sinusoidal sweeps and stepped position commands, with position or torque traces to inspect the response.
    • Integration groundwork: serial communication with CRC validation and separate per-motor command and feedback state on a shared connection. A simulation mode supports interface development without connected hardware.

    View the J288 control-bench repository on GitHub.
